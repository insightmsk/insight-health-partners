About Insight Health Partners

Clinician-led. NHS experienced. Built for better care.

We bring deep frontline knowledge together with clinical leadership, governance and service transformation expertise.

Credibility grounded in clinical practice.

Insight Health Partners Ltd was founded by four clinicians who understand diagnostic and community services from the inside.

The founders have more than 50 years of combined NHS experience. All founders are qualified musculoskeletal sonographers and work in Advanced Practice and Consultant-level clinical roles.

Their collective experience spans radiology, emergency medicine, orthopaedics, rheumatology, primary care and community services—alongside clinical leadership, CQC governance, education and service transformation.

CQC registration is currently in progress.
